# Validation: what the simulation covers and what only hardware can prove

## Already validated (off-device)

| What | How | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Shot detector logic | `firmware/sim/simulate.py`: Monte Carlo sessions at 3.2 kHz ground truth, through the same `shot_detect.c`, an IDLE/ACTIVE pipeline and a LIS2DE12 sensor model (±16 g clip, 187.5 mg LSB, no anti-alias filter = worst case) | See README (numbers regenerated by the final run) |
| Firmware | Zephyr 4.1 build, custom board, release and log+assert variants | 0 warnings |
| Schematic | KiCad netlist export vs `design.py` (`check_netlist.py`) | 80/80 pin assignments |
| PCB | Freerouting + KiCad DRC | 0 violations, 0 unconnected |
| Balance | CadQuery mass properties, tungsten trim solver | COM < 0.01 mm off axis (estimated masses) |

The simulation validates **logic and robustness margins**, not the physics of your bow. The signal model (release shock of 8–40 g, 15–80° rotation into the sling) is an assumption.

## On-device checklist

Do these in order: each step de-risks the next. **Bold** items are blockers.

### 1. Bench, cap off (programmer on the Tag-Connect)
- [ ] **First boot:** the LED blinks 200 ms, the puck advertises as ShotPuck, and the UICR REGOUT0 write causes one extra reset.
- [ ] **VDD measures 3.0 V** (REG0 programmed) and the accelerometer answers (no 1 Hz error blinking).
- [ ] **Standby current** with JP1 cut: IDLE and not advertising should be ≤ 8 µA, the target for the battery-life estimate. ACTIVE at 400 Hz should be about 60–100 µA. Advertising at 500 ms should add about 10–15 µA.
- [ ] **Charging:** about 50 mA (from R1 = 20k), and termination at 4.20 V ±0.75 %. Confirm both against the CP1654 datasheet limits before first charge.
- [ ] **CHG_STAT and VBUS flags** in STATUS follow the cable.
- [ ] **Charge inhibit:** freezer at −5 °C sets CHG_INHIBIT and the charge current drops to about 0. Warm to 20 °C and charging resumes.
- [ ] **Low-battery cut-off:** with a bench supply on the cell pads below 3.3 V, the puck enters System OFF (current under 1 µA). **Attaching the charger must wake it** (nRF52833 VBUS wake from System OFF: verify, it is load-bearing).

### 2. Assembled puck
- [ ] **BLE range through the cap**, with the puck on the riser and the phone in a pocket about 1–2 m away, and at 10 m. If the link is weak, raise TX power (`CONFIG_BT_CTLR_TX_PWR_PLUS_4=y`) or check the antenna keep-out against the aluminium base.
- [ ] Weigh each finished puck and compare with `mechanical/out/mass_report.txt`. Re-run `puck.py` with measured part masses before cutting the tungsten.
- [ ] Spin test: roll the puck on a flat surface or balance it on a pin. There should be no preferred rest position.
- [ ] Magnetic connector hold, and that it cannot mate reversed.

### 3. Shooting: threshold tuning (the key step)
- [ ] Log **every EVENT** (accepted and rejected) in the app for at least 3 sessions and 100+ shots, noting any miss or false count by hand.
- [ ] Compare the shots' `peak_hf_mg`, `pre_still_ms` and `post_lf_mg` with the simulated 5th–95th percentile ranges printed by `simulate.py`.
  - If shot peaks sit near or below 6000 mg (light draw weight, heavy riser damping), lower `trig_hf_mg`. The weak-bow simulation set shows recall collapsing if you don't.
  - If `post_lf_mg` of real shots is below 250 mg (firm grip, no sling), lower `post_lf_min_mg`. Watch for more stand-knock false counts.
- [ ] Feed the logged feature vectors back into the simulation, or send them to me, and re-verify before changing the defaults in `shot_detect.c`.
- [ ] Non-shot checks, 20 each: bow set on the stand, knock on the stand, walking with the bow, drop onto grass, let-down.

### 4. Durability
- [ ] 2,000+ shots, then inspect: cell tab solder joints, connector solder, screw torque (nylon-patch M1.6), O-ring seat, trim pin adhesive.
- [ ] Count accuracy unchanged after the durability run.
- [ ] Battery life over 2–4 weeks of normal training vs the estimate in the README.
