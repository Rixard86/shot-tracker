# Board-level (hardware) contributors to IDLE current, and hardware changes for a next revision

Scope: rev 0B board as described by `hardware/design.py` (the netlist source), checked against the firmware's pin states where a hardware path depends on them. Repository citations are `file:line` relative to the repository root. IDLE here means: no USB-C cable, accelerometer in LPM1 at 15 Hz with wake-up on INT1, gyroscope off, CPU asleep, advertising stopped, RTC on the internal RC oscillator.

## 1. What is connected to the cell / VBAT / VDD / VBUS rails, with which values? Is there a battery-measurement divider?

### Takeaway
The board has **no resistor divider at all on the cell**: battery voltage is read inside the nRF52840 (SAADC input VDDH/5). With no cable, the cell feeds only four DC loads: the nRF52840 on VDDH, the BQ29700 (through R7), the MCP73831's reverse-leakage path, and (through REG0 → VDD) the LSM6DSV32X. Every resistor on the board is either on the USB side (dead without a cable) or in series with a gate or a GPIO held at 0 V. **One exception:** R3 (100 kΩ, CHG_INH to GND) carries 3.0 V / 100 kΩ = **30 µA whenever the firmware drives CHG_INH high, and the firmware's temperature gate can do that with no cable present** (die temperature ≤ 0 °C or ≥ 45 °C).

- No external battery divider; battery voltage comes from the SAADC's internal VDDH/5 input ("No regulator, no battery divider (internal VDDH/5), fewer leaks") — [README.md:61](README.md); [firmware/src/power.c:7](firmware/src/power.c) ("Battery voltage: SAADC internal VDDH/5 input, no external divider"); input `NRF_SAADC_VDDHDIV5` at [firmware/src/power.c:64](firmware/src/power.c)
- No thermistor/NTC: charging is gated by the nRF die temperature (<1 °C / >44 °C) through Q1/R1/R2/R3 — [README.md:62](README.md); [hardware/design.py:20-23](hardware/design.py)
- Complete list of resistors (design.py): R1 47k PROG→PROG_SW (Q1A drain); R2 1M VIN→Q1_GATE; R3 100k CHG_INH→GND; R4 1k LED_IO→LED_A; R7 330R VBAT→PCM_BAT (BQ29700 supply filter, with C8 100n); R8 2.2k PCM_VM→GND; R9 1k VIN_RAW→VBUS_SNS (C9 100n); R10/R11 5.1k CC1/CC2→GND — [hardware/design.py:57-64, 92-99, 107-110](hardware/design.py)

- LED D4 (XL-1005SYGC, 0402) from LED_IO (P0.13) through R4 1k to GND; the pin is configured `GPIO_OUTPUT_INACTIVE` (low) — [hardware/design.py:63-64, 71-72](hardware/design.py); [firmware/src/led.c:95](firmware/src/led.c)

So no grounded pin fights an internal pull-up.

- VBUS sensing (R9/C9 → nRF VBUS pin) costs ~24 µA only while a cable is present (from the USB side, not the cell) — [README.md:85](README.md)

### Inferences
- **Battery divider current at 4.2 V: 0 µA** (there is none). This is already the best case; any future divider is a regression (see section 4).
- **R3 is a 30 µA leak in cold or hot idle** (3.0 V / 100 kΩ). An archer training outdoors at ≤ 0 °C, or a puck left in a hot car (die ≥ 45 °C), would idle at roughly 45 µA instead of ~15 µA, i.e. about three times the drain. Fix options: firmware drives CHG_INH only while VBUS is present (0 µA, no hardware change); or hardware raises R3 to 1 MΩ (3 µA when asserted) or more. R3 only has to hold Q1B's gate low while the MCU is dead, so a much larger value is acceptable (DMN63D8LDW gate-leakage figure not checked: see Gaps). When CHG_INH is high, Q1B also pulls Q1_GATE low, so R2 (1 MΩ from VIN) is loaded, but without a cable VIN is only fed by the MCP73831's µA-level reverse leakage, so R2 adds nothing measurable.
- Paths that carry **no DC current in IDLE** by topology: R1 (PROG, charger off), R2 (VIN ≈ 0 V), R4/D4 (GPIO low, LED unbiased), R7 (carries only the BQ29700's own supply current: 4 µA × 330 Ω = 1.3 mV drop), R8 (V– sits at ≈ VSS while both FETs are on), R9/C9/R10/R11/D3 (USB side; dead without a cable), D2 (pull-up off, STAT high-impedance), I2C pull-ups (lines idle high, nothing pulls them low), ACC_INT1 (push-pull driven by the IMU into a CMOS input), JP2 (open pads), SWD pads.
- **D1 and D3 are not separate paths**: the only source that can reach VIN/VIN_RAW without a cable is the MCP73831's reverse leakage, so D1's reverse leakage, D3's TVS leakage and the nRF VBUS-pin input current are all in series with, and bounded by, the MCP73831 IDISCHARGE figure (section 2).
- Conditional risk: the JP2 pull-up stays on permanently (`GPIO_PULL_UP` in the DT spec, [firmware/boards/shotpuck/shotpuck/shotpuck_nrf52840.dts:40](firmware/boards/shotpuck/shotpuck/shotpuck_nrf52840.dts)). If the bare, mask-free 0.4 mm gap is ever bridged by moisture, flux residue or debris, it draws up to 3.0 V / ~13 kΩ ≈ 230 µA. Moving JP2 to the bottom side (rev 0C item 1, [HANDOVER.md:386](HANDOVER.md)) reduces exposure; a firmware option is to enable the pull-up only when sampling.
- The IMU interrupt uses `GPIO_INT_LEVEL_ACTIVE` ([firmware/src/accel.c:99, 110](firmware/src/accel.c)). In Zephyr's nRF GPIO driver, level interrupts use the pin SENSE / PORT event rather than a GPIOTE IN channel. That matters because Nordic lists 17.37 µA for "wake on GPIOTE input (event mode)" against 2.36 µA for "wake on GPIOTE PORT event" ([nRF52840 PS sleep table](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/ps_nrf52840/page/_tmp/nrf52840/autodita/current/parameters.i_sleep.html)). So the current wiring avoids a ~15 µA penalty, and any future wake input (e.g., a second sensor) must also use SENSE/level or PORT. The Zephyr driver behaviour is from prior knowledge and was not re-checked in source.

### Gaps
- DMN63D8LDW (Q1) gate leakage was not checked, so the largest safe R3 value is not confirmed from a datasheet.
- MLCC insulation-resistance leakage of C2 (10 µF), C3/C4 (4.7 µF) at 3–4.2 V was not sourced; it is normally nA-level, but spec minimums for high-value X5R parts can allow sub-µA.
- Whether firmware samples JP2 only at boot or continuously was not checked (only the DT pull-up flag).

## 2. Datasheet quiescent/leakage figures per part, and the resulting IDLE budget

### Takeaway
At typical values the board's IDLE current is about **15 µA**: IMU ≈ 7 µA (46 %), BQ29700 4 µA (27 %), nRF52840 System ON with RTC on LFRC ≈ 2.8 µA (19 %), LFRC calibration ≈ 1 µA (7 %), MCP73831 reverse leakage ≈ 0.2 µA (1 %). FETs, diodes, the LED and resistors add essentially nothing at typical values. At datasheet maxima the total reaches about 20 µA. That agrees with the README/HANDOVER estimates of 14–17 µA.

### Cited Findings

IPower_down at BAT = V– = 1.5 V: **0.1 µA**. Family feature line: "NORMAL mode ICC = 4µA … Shutdown Iq = 100nA". BQ29700 thresholds: OVP 4.275 V, UVP 2.800 V, OCC −0.100 V, OCD 0.100 V, SCD 0.5 V. COUT/DOUT VOH 3.4–3.7 V at −30 µA. V– pull-up RV–D 100/300/550 kΩ (active only after over-discharge); V– current sink IV–S 8/24 µA (not active in power-down / over-discharge) — [TI BQ2970x datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq2970.pdf)

- Weekly effect for the usage example (162 h IDLE, 6 h ACTIVE at 0.85 mA): IDLE 162 h × 15 µA ≈ 2.4 mAh/week (2.75 mAh at the 17 µA estimate); ACTIVE ≈ 5.1 mAh/week; cell self-discharge ≤ 0.45 mAh/week (section 3). IDLE is therefore about 30 % of weekly drain, and **each 1 µA of IDLE current ≈ 0.16 mAh/week ≈ 2 % of the weekly budget**. Total ≈ 8 mAh/week → ≈ 8 weeks from 65 mAh (ideal, to empty; the 3.3 V cut-off leaves some capacity unused).
- The IMU's datasheet power-down floor (3 µA) means its LPM1 operation at 15 Hz costs only ≈ 3.9 µA above the floor. The remaining 3 µA can be removed only by cutting its supply (a load switch or a GPIO-powered rail), never by register settings.
- The nRF's high-voltage-mode overhead is small (≈ 0.3 µA System ON, ≈ 0.55 µA System OFF at 5 V on VDDH). At the cell's 3.7–4.2 V it is probably a little lower (no table row at those voltages).

### Gaps
- No datasheet maximum for LSM6DSV32X LPM1 or IddPD (typical only); the interpolated 6.9 µA at 15 Hz is an estimate, since the current need not scale linearly with ODR.
- No nRF52840 sleep-current figures for VDDH at 3.7–4.2 V, and none for REG0 DC/DC against LDO in System ON idle. The PS lists only 5 V VDDH rows.
- The LFRC calibration cost (17 ms × 460 µA) is a forum figure for the nRF52832, not a PS figure for the nRF52840; the real calibration rate depends on the Zephyr 4.1 defaults and temperature drift.
- BAT54J (Nexperia) and XL-1005SYGC reverse-leakage specs were not fetched; by topology they do not add IDLE current on this board.
- None of these figures is measured on the board; VALIDATION's standby-current test (JP1 cut) is still open — [README.md:212-214](README.md).

## 3. Cell self-discharge of the LIR1254/LIR1255 as a µA equivalent

### Takeaway
The cell's datasheet allows **at most 3 % per month**, which for 65 mAh is **≤ 1.95 mAh/month ≈ ≤ 2.7 µA equivalent**: comparable to the nRF52840's whole sleep current, and a hard floor that no board change removes.

### Cited Findings
- The project's cell is EEMB's LIR1255 (65 mAh nominal, 60 mAh minimum; CC/CV 4.20 V, end at 0.05C; ≥ 300 cycles to 80 %); "Self-discharge at most 3 % per month", from the EEMB specification ZJQM-RD-SPC-H2400 — [EEMB LIR1255 / "LIR1254" datasheet (scanned PDF)](https://www.opensourceinstruments.com/Electronics/Data/LIR1254.pdf), as recorded in the project's datasheet check of 2026-11-13 (the scan's text layer is partial; I did not re-open the PDF myself)

### Inferences
- 0.03 × 65 mAh = 1.95 mAh per month; ÷ 730 h ≈ **2.67 µA** (upper bound at the datasheet limit; the actual rate is usually lower at room temperature and higher when warm).
- Per week ≤ 0.45 mAh, about 5–6 % of the weekly budget in the usage example. If the board's IDLE drops to ~3 µA after the changes in section 4, self-discharge becomes about half of all standby losses.
- For storage, self-discharge dominates any good ship mode: with a 50 nA disconnect the cell still loses ≤ 3 %/month.

### Gaps
- The datasheet's storage temperature and conditions for the 3 %/month figure were not confirmed (scanned PDF not re-read).

## 4. Which hardware changes give the biggest IDLE saving per effort, and which need firmware changes?

### Takeaway
In order of saving per effort: (1) **stop the R3/CHG_INH leak** (0 to 30 µA, conditional; firmware one-liner or a resistor value); (2) **swap the BQ29700 for a sub-µA protection IC** such as ABLIC S-82M1A/S-82N1x (≈ −3.4 µA typ, no firmware); (3) **fit a 32.768 kHz crystal on the free module pads 13/14** (≈ −1.5 µA, one Kconfig change); (4) **move wake detection to a nano-power accelerometer and power-gate the LSM6DSV32X** (≈ −6.7 µA, the largest saving, but major firmware work and re-validation of wake behaviour). Things to avoid: permanent dividers, a battery gauge, chargers with a µA-level battery-side Iq, and always-on load switches.

### Cited Findings

- The project notes the module has no 32.768 kHz crystal and uses the RC — [README.md:63](README.md); [HANDOVER.md:287](HANDOVER.md)
- **Nano-power wake sensor:** ADXL367: 180 nA in motion-activated wake-up mode, 0.89 µA at 100 Hz ODR (2.0 V supply), 40 nA standby, 1.1–3.6 V supply (product page and datasheet Rev. 0 as summarised by search; test conditions not seen) — [Analog Devices ADXL367](https://www.analog.com/en/products/adxl367.html); [ADXL367 datasheet (mirror)](https://cdn.sparkfun.com/assets/2/e/1/8/6/adxl367.pdf)
- **Load switch:** TI TPS22916 (B/C/CN versions): shutdown current 10 nA typ / 100 nA max (−40 to 85 °C); quiescent current when on 0.5 µA typ / 1.0 µA max; active-low variants 100 nA typ / 300 nA max in shutdown (via search summary of SLVSDO5F) — [TI TPS22916 datasheet](https://ti.com/lit/pdf/SLVSDO5); [TI product page](https://www.ti.com/product/TPS22916)
- **Dividers and the SAADC:** the nRF52840 SAADC's longest acquisition time (40 µs) allows at most 800 kΩ source resistance; VDDHDIV5 needs ≥ 10 µs. Nordic engineers note that with higher source resistance the readings come out too low unless a capacitor is added at the pin — [nRF52840 PS, SAADC](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/ps_nrf52840/page/saadc.html); [Nordic DevZone thread 84038](https://devzone.nordicsemi.com/f/nordic-q-a/84038/max-source-resistance-value-of-saadc-in-nrf52840)
- The planned rev 0C power path (load sharing, AN1149 style: P-FET + Schottky) makes `VDDHDIV5` read the USB rail while plugged in, so a "cell divider into a spare SAADC pin" is listed as one option. HANDOVER also warns: "Pick a low-leakage Schottky: on the cell its reverse leakage into VIN drains the cell in System OFF" — [HANDOVER.md:394-399](HANDOVER.md)
- **Battery gauge (rev 0C item 9):** MAX17048 23 µA active, 3–4 µA hibernate (≈ +20–27 % idle); MAX17260 ≈ 5 µA (≈ +30–36 %) — [HANDOVER.md:409-418](HANDOVER.md)
- **Charger with ship mode / power path:** BQ25180 battery quiescent current 3–3.5 µA (VIN < UVLO or floating, watchdog and push-button disabled, I2C functional), 4–5 µA with the push-button enabled; "15-nA" shutdown/ship figure with VIN = 0 V. The datasheet's ship-mode row with push-button wake shows 3.2 µA typ / 4.5 µA max, which conflicts with TI's SLYT842 note saying ship mode is 15 nA (search summary of SLUSE99C) — [TI BQ25180 product page](https://www.ti.com/product/BQ25180); [BQ25180 datasheet](https://ti.com/document-viewer/BQ25180/datasheet); [TI SLYT842](https://ti.com/document-viewer/lit/html/SLYT842/GUID-420F3B4A-4432-4FA7-A743-80A5AD7CDC85)
- Rev 0C reversed-cell P-FET: gate to GND through ~100 kΩ, in series with JP1; a blue LED could run from VCCH through an N-FET — [HANDOVER.md:401-408](HANDOVER.md)

### Inferences
Ranked options (typical savings against the ≈ 15 µA IDLE; 1 µA ≈ 0.16 mAh/week ≈ 2 % of the weekly budget):

- **Things that would raise IDLE current** (relevant to the rev 0C list):
  - A **permanent cell divider** for the rev 0C power path: 1 MΩ + 1 MΩ draws 4.2 V / 2 MΩ = 2.1 µA (+14 % IDLE); 10 MΩ total draws 0.42 µA but is far above the SAADC's 800 kΩ limit, so it needs a hold capacitor (e.g. ~100 nF) and slow sampling. A **switched divider** (high-side P-FET with an N-FET level shifter, e.g. the dual-N DMN63D8LDW already in the BOM plus one P-FET) brings it back to ≈ 0 µA plus FET leakage. The divider-free alternative is to read the cell with VDDHDIV5 only while VBUS is absent (as today) and use STAT/timers while charging (firmware only).
  - The rev 0C **load-sharing Schottky** sits reverse-biased from the cell-side load node to VIN in IDLE. Its reverse leakage at ≈ 4 V adds directly to IDLE, so choose a part specified at low VR (or an ideal-diode / P-FET OR-ing).
  - A **battery gauge**: +3–5 µA (+20–36 %).
  - **Always-on load switches**: +0.5 µA typ each (TPS22916 Iq while on).
- Effectively zero-cost rev 0C items: the reversed-cell P-FET (its gate resistor carries only IGSS), extra GPIO-driven LEDs (0 µA with the GPIO low), and a blue LED through an N-FET from VCCH (only that FET's IDSS, nA typ; choose a low-IDSS part).
- Combining options 2 + 3 + 4 brings typical IDLE from ≈ 15 µA to ≈ 3.3 µA (nRF ≈ 2.3 µA after removing LFRC overhead, protector 0.6 µA, wake sensor 0.18 µA, charger 0.2 µA). For the usage example, IDLE drops from ≈ 2.4 to ≈ 0.55 mAh/week and total drain from ≈ 8.0 to ≈ 6.1 mAh/week (−24 %, ≈ 8 → ≈ 10.5 weeks). Options 2 + 3 alone (low effort): ≈ 15 → ≈ 10 µA, ≈ −0.8 mAh/week (≈ −10 % total).
- Hardware-only (no firmware): options 1 (R3 value), 2, and 5. A one-line or Kconfig change: options 1 (firmware variant) and 3. Substantial firmware: option 4, and option 6 (divider/VDD sensing).

## 5. Is a "ship/storage mode" possible with the current hardware, and what would one cost on the next revision?

### Takeaway
**Yes, partially, in firmware only:** System OFF with the IMU in power-down draws about **8 µA** (nRF ≈ 0.6–0.95 µA + IMU 3 µA + BQ29700 4 µA + MCP73831 0.15–0.25 µA). Adding the cell's ≤ 2.7 µA self-discharge, a full 65 mAh cell lasts about **8 months** to empty. The firmware already enters exactly this state at the low-battery cut-off, and wakes only on VBUS. A true disconnect is impossible on rev 0B because the BQ29700 has no enable pin and enters its 0.1 µA power-down only after an under-voltage trip at 2.8 V. On the next revision, an S-82N1B (protector with a power-saving pin, 50 nA max) or a load switch latched by the nRF and released by VBUS would bring storage drain to ≈ 0.05–4 µA.

### Cited Findings
- Low-battery cut-off: 3.3 V on three readings in a row, then System OFF until a charger is attached — [README.md:114](README.md). Before `power_off_until_charger()` the firmware calls `accel_off()` — [firmware/src/main.c:529-531, 548-549](firmware/src/main.c) — which writes CTRL1/CTRL2 = 0 (accelerometer and gyroscope ODR off = power-down) and disables INT1 routing — [firmware/src/accel.c:157-166](firmware/src/accel.c). CHG_INH is forced low and STAT's pull-up removed before `sys_poweroff()`; wake relies on VBUS (USBDETECTED) — [firmware/src/power.c:226-235](firmware/src/power.c)
- "VBUS wake from System OFF" is still an open validation blocker — [README.md:218](README.md); [HANDOVER.md:426](HANDOVER.md)
- nRF52840 System OFF, no RAM retention: 0.40 µA (3 V on VDD), **0.95 µA with 5 V on VDDH, REG0 = 3.3 V**; full RAM retention 1.86 µA — [nRF52840 PS sleep table](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/ps_nrf52840/page/_tmp/nrf52840/autodita/current/parameters.i_sleep.html). The BMD-340's VCC (REG0 output) can supply at most 1 mA in System OFF in high-voltage mode, so VDD and the IMU stay powered — [u-blox BMD-340 data sheet](https://content.u-blox.com/sites/default/files/BMD-340_DataSheet_UBX-19033353.pdf)
- LSM6DSV32X power-down: 3 µA — [ST LSM6DSV32X datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/lsm6dsv32x.pdf)
- BQ29700: 4 µA typ / 5.5 µA max normal; 0.1 µA in power-down, entered only after over-discharge (UVP 2.800 V) and released when a charger is connected — [TI BQ2970x datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq2970.pdf)
- MCP73831 battery discharge current with no input: 0.15–0.25 µA typ, 2 µA max — [Microchip MCP73831 datasheet](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/MCP73831-Family-Data-Sheet-DS20001984H.pdf)
- S-82N1B: the PS pin "will activate the power-saving function and disable battery discharge", with IC current at 50 nA max; the page does not say how the state is released — [ABLIC S-82M1/S-82N1](https://www.ablic.com/en/semicon/products/power-management-ic/lithium-ion-battery-protection-ic/s-82m1-82n1/)
- TPS22916: 10 nA typ / 100 nA max shutdown, 0.5 µA typ on — [TI TPS22916](https://ti.com/lit/pdf/SLVSDO5)
- BQ25180: ship/shutdown 15 nA vs 3.2 µA typ with push-button wake (conflicting figures); battery-only Iq 3–3.5 µA — [TI BQ25180 datasheet](https://ti.com/document-viewer/BQ25180/datasheet); [TI SLYT842](https://ti.com/document-viewer/lit/html/SLYT842/GUID-420F3B4A-4432-4FA7-A743-80A5AD7CDC85)

### Inferences
- **Rev 0B storage mode (firmware only):** reuse the cut-off path as a "storage" command (e.g. over BLE, or JP2 held at boot). Drain ≈ 0.6–0.95 µA (nRF System OFF, VDDH) + 3 µA (IMU PD) + 4 µA (BQ29700) + 0.2 µA (MCP73831) ≈ **7.8–8.2 µA**, ≈ 6 mAh/month. With ≤ 2.7 µA self-discharge: 65 mAh / ≈ 10.7 µA ≈ 6,000 h ≈ **8 months** from full to empty (ideal), against ≈ 6 weeks at the normal 15 µA IDLE plus self-discharge with no use. Parts cost: none. It depends on VBUS wake from System OFF, which is unverified on hardware.
- **Storage risk:** after the 3.3 V System OFF cut-off the board still draws ≈ 8 µA. The cell then drifts down to the BQ29700's UVP (2.8 V), after which the PCM draws 0.1 µA while self-discharge continues. A puck left flat for months can therefore go well below 2.8 V; a lower storage drain lengthens that margin.
- **Next-revision ship-mode options and their cost:**
  - **A. S-82N1B with its PS pin driven by an nRF GPIO:** the whole board is disconnected; storage drain ≈ 0.05 µA (IC) + leakage, so self-discharge alone dominates. Parts: replaces U4 (no extra part). Also delivers option 2's −3.4 µA in normal IDLE. Unknowns: how PS is released (likely charger connection) and how a GPIO-held PS input behaves once the MCU loses power (the pin needs a defined default through a resistor).
  - **B. Load switch (TPS22916) between VBAT and U1 VCCH**, EN diode-ORed from VBUS (sets it on at plug-in) and an nRF GPIO (holds it on while running; releasing the GPIO = ship). Storage ≈ 4 µA (BQ29700) + 0.2 µA + 0.01 µA ≈ 4.2 µA with the BQ29700, or ≈ 0.8 µA with an S-82M1. Cost: a WCSP/SOT-23 switch + 1–2 diodes/resistors, plus **+0.5 µA always-on Iq in normal IDLE (+3 %)** and its RON in series with radio/LED peaks. It would also power down the IMU (removing its 3 µA floor in storage).
  - **C. Charger with ship mode and power path (BQ25180 class):** covers the rev 0C load-sharing item and gives a 15 nA shutdown, but adds ≈ 3 µA battery-side Iq in normal operation (against the MCP73831's 0.2 µA). The ship-mode figure conflicts between TI documents. Net worse for daily IDLE unless the puck spends much of its life in storage.
  - **D. IMU-only load switch** (if the IMU stays the wake source in normal use): saves 3 µA only in System OFF/storage, costs +0.5 µA (switch Iq) in IDLE. Worthwhile only if storage matters more than daily IDLE.
- Best combination for both goals: **S-82N1B (normal IDLE −3.4 µA, ship ≈ 50 nA) + crystal (−1.5 µA)**. Together they need one GPIO, two small passives and the crystal, and only small firmware changes (Kconfig + a storage command).

### Gaps
- S-82N1B PS-pin release condition, polarity and default state were not found on the product page; the datasheet still has to be read.
- nRF52840 VBUS wake from System OFF is not yet validated on this board.
- BQ25180's true ship-mode current conflicts between the datasheet table and TI's SLYT842 (3.2 µA with push-button wake vs 15 nA); not resolved.
- No source for the nRF52840 System OFF current at VDDH 3.7–4.2 V (only 3 V VDD and 5 V VDDH rows).
